# Alcohol-Related Deaths

The leading identifiable cause of death in fraternity and sorority incidents.

<Callout intent="warning" title="76 documented incidents">
  **76 incidents** documented. **70 (92%)** were Greek organization-related. Alcohol-related fatalities account for roughly one in four documented incidents and have climbed sharply since the 1970s. Nearly all involve organized fraternity or sorority events where excessive drinking is encouraged or coerced.
</Callout>
