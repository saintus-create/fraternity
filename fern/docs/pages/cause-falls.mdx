# Falls

Deaths from falls off roofs, balconies, windows, stairs, gorges, and other elevated surfaces.

<Callout intent="warning" title="89 documented incidents">
  **89 incidents** documented. **70 (79%)** were Greek organization-related. Falls are the largest single cause category in the database. Many occur at fraternity-owned structures, and the [obfuscation research](/obfuscation-patterns) shows these deaths are frequently misclassified as simple accidents.
</Callout>
