# Legal Trends & Data

<Badge intent="info">1980–2023</Badge> <Badge intent="success">Courts rejecting "no duty of care"</Badge> <Badge intent="error">Felony charges for participants</Badge> <Badge intent="warning">Systematic obfuscation</Badge>

## Shifting Legal Landscape

Courts are increasingly rejecting fraternity and university attempts to avoid responsibility:

| Trend | Impact |
|-------|--------|
| Rejection of "no duty of care" arguments | Organizations held responsible for foreseeable harm |
| Piercing organizational liability shields | National organizations held liable for local chapter actions |
| Criminal prosecution of individual members | Hazing participants facing felony charges |
| University liability for known hazards | Institutions liable when they had knowledge of prior violations |
| Family-driven legislative reform | New state hazing laws creating criminal penalties |

## Key Findings

<Callout intent="error" title="Systematic Obfuscation">
  Clear evidence that fraternities and universities routinely attempt to hide fraternity connections in death cases across all documented time periods.
</Callout>

<Callout intent="success" title="Accountability is increasing">
  Courts are increasingly rejecting fraternity/university attempts to avoid responsibility, and family advocacy is driving new laws and policies.
</Callout>

- **Legal Redress** — Courts increasingly rejecting fraternity/university attempts to avoid responsibility
- **Family Advocacy** — Growing momentum for transparency and accountability, with families driving legislative change
- **Legislative Response** — New laws and policies emerging directly from family advocacy efforts
- **Institutional Pattern** — Consistent delay in medical care and evidence preservation across cases spanning decades
