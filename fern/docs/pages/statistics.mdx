# Statistics Overview

This page provides a statistical summary of the collected fraternity and sorority related incident data.

## Data Summary

The dataset encompasses **decades of documented incidents** across various educational institutions in the United States.

### Time Period Coverage

| Period | Range |
|--------|-------|
| Earliest Year | 1928 |
| Latest Year | 2026 |
| Total Years Spanned | ~98 years |

### Student Levels

The incidents involve two primary student levels:

- **College Students**: Majority of documented cases
- **High School Students**: Limited cases, primarily from earlier decades

### Incident Categories

The dataset includes various types of incidents:

- **Greek Organization Related**: Incidents directly related to fraternity/sorority activities
- **Non-Greek Organization Related**: Incidents not directly tied to Greek life
- **Multiple Victim Incidents**: Cases involving multiple individuals

### Geographic Distribution

Incidents are documented across multiple U.S. states and territories:

- **States**: California, Texas, Pennsylvania, Florida, New York, and many others
- **Federal Military**: Some incidents involving military institutions
- **Territories**: Puerto Rico

### Primary Causes of Death

Common causes of death documented in the dataset include:

1. **Alcohol Intoxication** - Most common cause in recent decades
2. **Falls** - Balcony falls, falls from heights
3. **Drowning** - Water-related incidents
4. **Head Trauma** - Blunt force injuries
5. **Vehicle Accidents** - Traffic-related fatalities
6. **Other Causes** - Various other circumstances

### Greek Organizations

The dataset references numerous Greek organizations including:

- Fraternities (Phi Delta Theta, Sigma Alpha Epsilon, Kappa Sigma, etc.)
- Sororities (Alpha Kappa Alpha, Pi Beta Phi, etc.)
- Marching Bands and other organizations

> **Note**: For the complete dataset, please refer to the [Victim Data](/docs/pages/victim-data) page.

## Data Integrity

Some entries in the dataset may have:

- Missing or unknown years (marked as year 0)
- Unidentified victims
- Incomplete information due to historical record limitations
