# Statistics

A quantitative analysis of **294 documented incidents** involving **306 total victims** spanning from 1873 to 2026.

<Badge intent="error">294 incidents</Badge> <Badge intent="warning">306 victims</Badge> <Badge intent="info">1873–2026</Badge> <Badge intent="note">72.4% Greek-related</Badge>

<Note title="Methodology">
  Counts on this page are drawn from the deduplicated incident dataset. Cause-of-death categories are approximate and some incidents have overlapping causes; incidents with an unknown year or state are counted in an "Unknown" category. See [Methodology & Data Sources](/hazing-overview) for how the data was compiled.
</Note>
