# Physical Trauma & Abuse

Deaths resulting from physical violence, beatings, and blunt force trauma during hazing.

<Callout intent="warning" title="26 documented incidents">
  **26 incidents** documented. **19 (73%)** were Greek organization-related. This category covers deaths from beatings, blunt force, and head injuries sustained during hazing or at Greek events, including falls whose recorded cause is head trauma.
</Callout>
