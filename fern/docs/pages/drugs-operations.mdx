# Fraternity Drug Busts: Operations

This report provides a quantified assessment of fraternity drug enforcement actions, drug seizure quantities, and statistical analysis of substance use within Greek life organizations at college campuses across the United States. The research covers major enforcement operations from **2008 to 2025**.

<Callout intent="info">
  Fraternity drug incidents represent a relatively small number of total arrests, but often involve disproportionately high drug quantities and organized distribution networks, leading to significant federal enforcement attention.
</Callout>

<Accordion title="Operation Sudden Fall (2008) — San Diego State University">

<Badge intent="error">96 arrests</Badge> <Badge intent="warning">75 SDSU students</Badge> <Badge intent="info">Joint DEA / SDSU</Badge> <Badge intent="note">7 fraternities infiltrated</Badge>

One of the largest campus drug busts in U.S. history, this joint DEA and San Diego State University operation was launched following the cocaine overdose death of a 19-year-old student in May 2007. The five-month investigation infiltrated **seven campus fraternities** and resulted in the arrest of **96 individuals**, including 75 San Diego State students.

### Drug Quantities Seized

| Drug Type | Quantity |
|-----------|----------|
| Cocaine | 4 pounds (1.8 kg) |
| Marijuana | 50 pounds (22.7 kg) |
| Hydroponic Plants | 48 plants |
| Ecstasy | 350 pills |
| Psilocybin Mushrooms | Unspecified quantity |
| Hash Oil | 30 vials |
| Methamphetamine | Unspecified quantity |
| Prescription Drugs | Various types |

Investigators discovered that in some fraternities, most of the members were involved in drug distribution. The operation revealed organized networks within Greek housing that extended beyond casual use into **commercial-scale distribution**. This case set a precedent for DEA involvement in campus drug enforcement.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="UNC/Duke Drug Ring (2020) — North Carolina Universities">

<Badge intent="error">21 federal charges</Badge> <Badge intent="warning">3 fraternities suspended</Badge> <Badge intent="warning">Kappa Sigma lease terminated</Badge> <Badge intent="info">$1.5M+ proceeds</Badge>

A multi-year federal investigation uncovered a sophisticated drug trafficking network spanning three major North Carolina universities: **University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill**, **Duke University**, and **Appalachian State University**. Twenty-one individuals faced federal charges.

### Network Drug Totals

| Drug Type | Quantity |
|-----------|----------|
| Marijuana | Over 1,000 pounds (453+ kg) |
| Cocaine | Several hundred kilograms |
| LSD | Large quantities |
| MDMA (Molly) | Large quantities |
| Psilocybin Mushrooms | Large quantities |

### Single Operation Seizure

| Drug Type | Quantity |
|-----------|----------|
| Marijuana | 148.75 pounds (67.5 kg) |
| Cocaine | 442 grams (0.44 kg) |
| Xanax Pills | 189 pills |

**Financial Data:** Total drug proceeds exceeded **$1.5 million** over the three-year investigation period. The network received approximately 90 kg (200 lbs) of cannabis and 2 kg of cocaine **per week** at peak operation.

Federal investigators found drug trafficking in and near three fraternity houses at UNC-Chapel Hill, leading to the suspension of three fraternities and the termination of the **Kappa Sigma** fraternity house lease.
