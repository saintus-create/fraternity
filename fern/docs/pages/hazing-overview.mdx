# Hazing Mortality: Overview & Statistics

This section covers the executive summary, overall statistics, data sources, and decade trends of our hazing mortality research.

<Callout intent="info" title="Executive Summary">
This comprehensive research report aggregates data on fraternity-related deaths and hazing incidents collected from multiple sources, including published datasets, news reports, legal documents, and institutional records. The research spans **1950 to 2025**, documenting over **300 hazing-related deaths** in the United States and providing detailed analysis of patterns, trends, and contributing factors.
</Callout>

## Data Sources & Methodology

<AccordionGroup>
<Accordion title="Primary Data Sources">

- **U.S. Hazing Deaths Dataset** — ~500+ hazing deaths spanning 1737–2022, with 396 records extracted and 314 high-quality records filtered for 1950–2025
- **Blunt Force Trauma Dataset** — 30+ additional death cases involving blunt force trauma
- **New Victim Reports** — 21 detailed modern case files (2004–2023) with comprehensive information
- **Pi Kappa Phi Chapter Analysis** — 116 chapter profiles with lifecycle data

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Research Limitations & Methodology">

**Data Collection Period**
- **Historical Dataset**: 1950–2025 (314 documented cases)
- **Modern Dataset**: 2004–2023 (21 detailed cases)
- **Chapter Analysis**: 1904–2025 (116 chapters tracked)

**Limitations**
Some entries in historical formats contain duplicates due to data sourcing from multiple independent compilations. The research team implemented cross-referencing and deduplication logic during data processing to minimize duplicate entries while maximizing comprehensive coverage.

</Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Decade Trends

| Decade | Incidents | Notes |
|--------|----------:|-------|
| 1950s–1960s | ~14 | Foundational hazing practices established |
| 1970s | 18 | Peak chartering period for many fraternities |
| 1980s | 25 | Growing awareness, limited documentation |
| 1990s | 28 | First major anti-hazing legislation passes |
| 2000s | 44 | Media attention increases, online reporting expands |
| 2010s | 77 | Highest documented decade on record |
| 2020s | 61+ | Ongoing; Stop Campus Hazing Act enacted (2024) |

<Callout intent="warn">
The 2010s have the highest incident count on record with **77 documented deaths**. The 2020s are currently on pace to match or exceed that figure.
</Callout>
