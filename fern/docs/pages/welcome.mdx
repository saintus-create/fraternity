# Fraternity & Sorority Incident Database

This project documents hazing-related fatalities across American institutions through a structured research record and a transparent documentation process.

<Callout intent="warning" title="Sensitive content">
  This site contains information about student deaths and other sensitive incidents. It is maintained for research, education, and accountability purposes. If you or someone you know is affected by hazing, see [Support Resources](/support).
</Callout>

## Research areas

Use the navigation to explore the chronology of incidents, causes, organizations, geography, case studies, institutional patterns, and enforcement records.

The project is designed to preserve the distinction between documented records, analytical conclusions, and material that still requires review. Records are not treated as conclusions merely because a name or incident appears in a source.

## Sources and provenance

The published research is assembled from public records, news reporting, court documents, law-enforcement records, and academic research. Methodology and provenance information are maintained alongside the relevant published material.

<Note>
  This documentation is a living research project. Published figures and classifications may change as records are reconciled, corrected, or supported by stronger documentation.
</Note>
