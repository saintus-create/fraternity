# The Cost of Hazing

While historical research indicates that **total fraternity and sorority hazing-related mortality exceeds 700 deaths** since the 1700s, this database meticulously tracks **396 highly-documented fatalities** from our modern and verified historical datasets. This site exists to make these numbers impossible to ignore.

<Callout intent="warning" title="Sensitive content">
  This site contains sensitive information about student deaths. It is maintained for research, education, and advocacy purposes. If you or someone you know is affected by hazing, see [Support Resources](/support).
</Callout>

## The Pattern

Our research reveals a disturbing acceleration. Hazing deaths are not declining — they are **increasing**, and the systems meant to prevent them are failing.

<AccordionGroup>
<Accordion title="Alcohol is the #1 identifiable killer — 25% of all deaths">

Alcohol-related fatalities account for approximately one in four documented incidents, with the rate climbing sharply since the 1970s. Nearly all involve organized fraternity or sorority events where excessive drinking is encouraged or coerced.

→ [Browse all alcohol-related deaths](/cause-alcohol)

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Falls are a major and underreported cause — 29% of all deaths">

Falls from roofs, balconies, windows, stairwells, gorges, and other heights account for the largest single cause category. Many occur at fraternity-owned structures where building safety is inadequate. Our [obfuscation research](/obfuscation-patterns) shows these are frequently misclassified as simple accidents.

→ [Browse all fall-related deaths](/cause-falls)

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Incidents are accelerating, not declining">

The 2010s saw **77 documented incidents** — more than any prior decade. The 2020s are on pace to match or exceed that, with **61 incidents** already recorded mid-decade. Despite new laws, Greek organization reform campaigns, and media attention, the rate of death continues to climb.

→ [View the full timeline](/timeline-20s)

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Systematic cover-ups are documented and proven">

Our [investigation into obfuscated cases](/obfuscation-patterns) identifies eight distinct patterns of institutional deception — from evidence destruction and delayed medical care to witness intimidation and external-factor blaming. Courts are increasingly rejecting these tactics.

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Drug networks operate within fraternity structures">

Major federal operations — including raids netting **1,000+ pounds of marijuana** and **hundreds of kilograms of cocaine** — have revealed that fraternity houses serve as organized distribution points. Greek organization members show **77% higher rates** of prescription drug abuse compared to non-Greek students.

→ [Read the full drug enforcement report](/drugs-operations)

</Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## About This Project

This database was created to:

- **Document** the full scope of hazing-related student deaths in America — going back to 1873
- **Expose** patterns of institutional cover-up and organizational negligence
- **Inform** policy discussions around the Stop Campus Hazing Act (2024) and fraternity reform
- **Support** researchers, families, and advocates working toward accountability
- **Honor** the memory of those lost to preventable incidents

<Note>
  All data is compiled from public records, news reports, court documents, law enforcement records, and academic research. Provided for informational and research purposes. See [Methodology & Data Sources](/hazing-overview) for how the datasets were compiled.
</Note>
