# Fraternity & Sorority Incident Database

A comprehensive, open-source database documenting hazing-related and student organization fatalities across American educational institutions from **1873 to present**.

<Callout intent="warn">
  This database contains sensitive information about student deaths. It is maintained for research, education, and advocacy purposes.
</Callout>

## Key Findings

<Accordion title="Alcohol is the #1 identifiable cause of death">
Alcohol-related deaths account for approximately 25% of all documented incidents, with the rate increasing sharply since the 1970s. Nearly all alcohol-related deaths are connected to Greek organization events.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Falls are a major and underreported cause">
Falls from roofs, balconies, windows, and gorges account for approximately 29% of all incidents. Many occur at fraternity house structures or during events where alcohol is involved.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Incidents are accelerating, not declining">
The 2010s saw 77 documented incidents — more than any prior decade. The 2020s are on pace to match or exceed that number, with 61 incidents already recorded mid-decade.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Over 72% of incidents involve Greek organizations">
213 of 294 documented incidents are directly linked to fraternity or sorority activities, pledging, hazing, or events.
</Accordion>

## About This Project

This database was created to:

- **Document** the scope and scale of hazing-related student deaths in America
- **Inform** policy discussions around fraternity and sorority reform
- **Support** researchers studying Greek life safety and institutional accountability
- **Honor** the memory of those lost to preventable incidents

> All data is provided for informational and research purposes only.
