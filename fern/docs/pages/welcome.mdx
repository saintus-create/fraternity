# Fraternity & Sorority Incident Database

A comprehensive, open-source database documenting hazing-related and student organization fatalities across American educational institutions from **1928 to present**.

<Callout intent="warn">
  This database contains sensitive information about student deaths. It is maintained for research, education, and advocacy purposes.
</Callout>

## Key Findings

- **Alcohol intoxication** is the leading cause of death, especially since the 1970s
- Over **60%** of documented incidents are related to Greek organizations
- **California, Texas, New York, and Pennsylvania** have the highest number of incidents
- Incidents have **accelerated significantly** since the late 1990s

## About This Project

This database was created to:

- **Document** the scope and scale of hazing-related student deaths in America
- **Inform** policy discussions around fraternity and sorority reform
- **Support** researchers studying Greek life safety and institutional accountability
- **Honor** the memory of those lost to preventable incidents

## Data Sources

The data is compiled from public records, news reports, court documents, and academic research. Some entries may contain incomplete information due to historical record limitations.

> All data is provided for informational and research purposes only.
