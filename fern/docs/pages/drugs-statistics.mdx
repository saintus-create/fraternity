## Substance Use Statistics: Greek Life vs. General Population

### Alcohol Consumption

| Metric | Greek Life | General College Population |
|--------|:----------:|:--------------------------:|
| Alcohol consumption (past year) | 92% | 72% |
| Binge drinking prevalence | 80% | 40% |
| Fraternity resident binge drinking | 88% | N/A |

### Risk Factors

| Risk Factor | Increase vs. General Population |
|-------------|:-------------------------------:|
| Fraternity residence and substance use | 2–3x higher |
| Prescription drug abuse (stimulants) | 77% higher |
| Binge drinking prevalence | 100% higher |
| Marijuana use (fraternity residents) | Highest prevalence category |

<Callout intent="warning" title="Residence is the strongest risk factor">
  Living in a fraternity house increases substance use risk by **2–3 times** compared to non-residential membership. Studies found that fraternity house residents had higher odds of alcohol use disorder symptoms extending into **early midlife**.
</Callout>

## Law Enforcement Response

### Enforcement Methods

- **Infiltration Operations** — Undercover agents placed within fraternity networks (SDSU: 5 months, 130+ purchases)
- **Joint Federal-State Operations** — DEA involvement for networks spanning multiple institutions
- **Asset Forfeiture** — Fraternity houses seized or leases terminated (UNC Kappa Sigma)

### Consequences

<Badge intent="success">100% conviction rate</Badge> <Badge intent="error">Prison sentences</Badge> <Badge intent="error">Chapter closures</Badge> <Badge intent="warning">Asset forfeiture</Badge>

<Callout intent="success" title="Enforcement outcomes">
  Federal involvement in fraternity drug cases correlates with **100% conviction rates** in documented operations.
</Callout>

Federal involvement in fraternity drug cases correlates with **100% conviction rates** in documented operations. Consequences include:

- Prison sentences ranging from mandatory minimums to multi-year terms
- Permanent closure of fraternity chapters
- Loss of university recognition and housing privileges
- Financial penalties and asset forfeiture
- Long-term criminal records
